KATANA

Stand in front of a camera, face a friend, and fight. Your body becomes a stickman, your hand becomes a 2 m sword. Slash to hit, cross blades to clash.

I have the camera I have the screen

How to play

  1. Prop a phone or laptop up facing both players, a few meters back, so your whole bodies are in view.
  2. Open /start on that device and tap Start camera. It shows a 4-digit code.
  3. Open /display on the TV or laptop you want to watch on, and type the code. The same device can do both if you open two tabs.
  4. Stand apart, one player on each side. When both of you are detected, the countdown begins.
  5. Swing your sword hand fast to slash. First to win two rounds wins the match.

Safety

Clear the space around you before you start. Use your bare hand, or a light foam item. Never swing a hard object near people, and keep your elbows in when you are close. Take breaks, matches are tiring.
